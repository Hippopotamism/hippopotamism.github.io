Hippopotamism

"chai me sabun"

Hippopotamism is an open-source religion. No priesthood, no fixed scripture, no final answer. Just a community repository of beliefs that anyone can fork, question, and improve.

The Core Idea

Not a dogma. A repository.

Hippopotamism ships with a default belief set, but nothing is frozen. What we believe today is simply the latest merged commit, always open to revision and always open to doubt.

🌊

No Fixed Dogma

There is no final, unchangeable holy text. Doctrine here is a draft, not a verdict.

🔓

Fully Open Source

Every teaching, ritual, and rule lives in the open. Read it, question it, or send a pull request. Belief is public infrastructure.

🧠

Critical Thinking First

Nothing is true here because it is old, or because someone in charge said so. We ask believers to think, not just to accept.

🤝

Built By Contribution

Hippopotamism grows only through its community. Every believer is also, potentially, an author of the next verse.

🦛

No Priesthood

No single person owns the truth. There is no clergy standing between a believer and their own reasoning.

⚖️

Change Is Expected

Beliefs update as better arguments arrive. Changing your mind is treated as a feature, not a failure.

Working Draft

The Five Commandments (so far)

Merged from the community. Numbered, not ranked, and open to a future pull request.

Bathe like a hippo

Submerge fully, cool off often, and do not apologize for taking up space in the water.

Question the changelog

No belief is above review. If something in the doctrine seems wrong, open an issue and say so.

Credit thy contributors

Every idea merged into the faith keeps the name of who proposed it. Belief has an author history.

Argue in good faith

Disagreement is welcome. Dismissing a claim without a reason is not.

Say the slogan with feeling

"Chai me sabun": a reminder that a religion can be sincere about its ideas and unserious about itself.

How To Contribute

Becoming a believer works the same way as improving the doctrine.

Step 1

Fork the Faith

Take the current beliefs as your starting point.

git clone hippopotamism
Step 2

Question Something

Sit with a teaching that does not quite hold up.

open an issue
Step 3

Propose a Belief

Write it down. A ritual, a rule, a new commandment.

git commit -m "faith"
Step 4

Merge With The Community

If it holds up to discussion, it becomes doctrine.

git merge --no-ff

All of it lives at github.com/Hippopotamism.
